Execution Proof

Proof Stories

Story 1: "From kickoff to a delivery team that owns it"

SITUATION
A new managed-services customer arrives with an environment that no delivery engineer has operated yet, and with expectations that have not been written down.
ACTION
Cody leads the kickoff, runs technical discovery, writes the implementation requirements and success criteria, names the risks, and owns the environment transition through to the delivery-team assignment.
CONSEQUENCE
The handoff is called only when the requirements, the success criteria, and the owner exist together; that agreement is what the delivery team receives, rather than a ticket and a hope.

Story 2: "An ambiguous production issue becomes a remediation plan"

SITUATION
A business-critical analytics platform is unstable across a clustered Windows and Linux environment, and the reported symptom is not where the cause is.
ACTION
Cody isolates the root cause, coordinates the stakeholders who each own part of the environment, and keeps the issue moving from investigation through remediation and into validation.
CONSEQUENCE
The work product is an actionable remediation plan and a validated change, communicated to both technical and non-technical audiences — the issue is not closed at the fix.
